# Future Architectural Direction

## Scope

This directory is reserved for accepted intended future capabilities and architectural direction. Such plans may constrain present design decisions even though the capability is not yet implemented.

## Important Boundary

Design documents describe intended future direction and **MUST NOT** be treated as descriptions of current system behavior.

Current facts belong in [`../domain/`](../domain/index.md). The [Architecture Map](../../ARCHITECTURE.md) is the entry point for understanding the present structure and navigating to either category.
